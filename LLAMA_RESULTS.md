# LLAMA_RESULTS: Llama 3.1 8B replication of the Gemma haggling study (branch `llama`)

**Code check (replaces the earlier limitation "harness not yet checked against stored Gemma scores"; DECISIONS.md L35-L39).** The harness parity check against the stored Gemma scores first failed its frozen per-case tolerance (up to 1.7e-2 vs 1e-3), because the harness read Gemma 2 logits after transformers' bf16 final soft-cap. After the owner-approved fix (last-position lm_head and a float32 soft-cap for soft-capped models, as the pre-refactor code did), harness plain and the nine stored adapters reproduce the stored A40 rescore to at most 3.3e-16 per case, with identical mean regret: **parity passes on the A40**. Against the A10G originals, per-case differences remain at 1.2e-2 to 5.8e-2 (the known GPU effect, HARDWARE_PARITY.md on `demo`), mean regret within 0.0035. The fix does not touch models without a final soft-cap: rescoring Llama plain, the wrong-rule arm and an examples-100 adapter with the fixed code is bit-identical to the stored records (88/88 cases each). Gemma-vs-Llama comparisons remain confounded by model-specific framing (system message, the "Answer: (" prefix), attention and, for the A10G column, the GPU, but no longer by the code path.

**Post-hoc change (owner decision, 2026-11-27, made after the data were seen; DECISIONS.md L18).** The readout check before step 4 was restated as format-only: pass if the mean letter mass with the "Answer: (" assistant prefix is at least 0.95 on the probe cases. The pre-registered check (agreement of the forced letter with Llama's free answer at least 0.7) failed and is now a **reported limitation**: the multiple-choice pick that every number below scores agrees with Llama's own free answer in only about half of the probe prompts. Reason given: Gemma's written offer matched its pick in about a third of cases (open node E1), so the old bar was stricter than Gemma ever met.

Every number below is generated by `tools/llama_report.py` from run records and the probe files it names; choices are in DECISIONS.md and LLAMA_PLAN.md.

## Summary of the tune results (hand-written after review gate G2; every number is backed in CLAIMS.yaml and checked by tools/check_claims.py)

All numbers are soft regret in coins on the tune cells (lower is better), computed from the **forced-choice letter read after "Answer: ("**, not from offers Llama writes freely. They are tune results only, with no test look. Comparisons with Gemma are confounded by model-specific framing; the harness code path itself now matches the stored Gemma scores (code check above).

- **Plain Llama is close to uniform guessing.** It scores 1.18, against 1.26 for picking at random and 0.214 for the model-free stated-cost rule. Its expected offer is too high on every case type. The frozen rule R1 is met, but uniform guessing would meet it too, so "fails the way Gemma did" is not claimed (Gemma's plain score on the same cells was 0.66).
- **Both written paragraphs lower regret, but so does a wrong rule.** Our method paragraph scores 0.77, Claude's lesson 0.93, neutral text 1.16 and the one-sentence wrong rule 0.61. The wrong rule helps at least as much as either paragraph. So the prompt results do not show that the paragraphs' content is what helps; the gain may be generic (lower offers, less letter-position bias).
- **LoRA on Claude's worked examples brings regret near zero on these in-family cells.** Examples-100 averages 0.048 over 3 seeds (Gemma 0.032); examples-25 scores 0.086 (one seed, worse than every examples-100 seed). For the controls, cheapest-label N=100 scores 0.43 (it collapses onto always-cheapest, so beating it is a weak test) and scrambled answers N=100 score 0.66. Against the stated-cost rule the margin is small. Training covers the same values, grids and wording as tune, so this is in-family fit, not generalisation.
- **The frozen rule R3 reads "not replicated".** Every examples-100 adapter fails the planted no-cost case (mass on the expected option 0.40, 0.13 and 0.36 for seeds 0-2, against a 0.9 bar); all other R3 conditions hold (labelled secondary). Gemma's examples-100 adapters, scored on the same planted cases through the harness, fail the same no-cost case (mass 0.20, 0.29 and 0.57), so this failure is shared with Gemma, not new in Llama.
- **Readout.** For plain Llama the forced-choice letter agrees with its own free answer on about half the probe prompts (post-hoc note above). With the examples-100 adapters loaded, agreement is 0.95, 0.90 and 0.95 on the same prompts: the trained models' written answers follow their forced-choice letter.
