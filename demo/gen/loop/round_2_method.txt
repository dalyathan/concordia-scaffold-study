Ignore usual market prices and ignore driving a hard bargain. If the seller states the farm cost, believe it and ignore the cost limits. Find the lowest listed price that is not below the cost; the seller accepts it. If the cost is below every listed price, this is the cheapest listed price. If this price is less than the resale value, offer it. If not, offer the cheapest listed price. If no cost is stated, any offer below the lowest possible farm cost is always refused. Find the number halfway between the lowest possible farm cost and the resale value, and round it down. If it is above the highest possible farm cost, use that highest cost instead. Offer the listed price equal to that number. If every listed price is above it, offer the cheapest; if every listed price is below it, offer the dearest.
