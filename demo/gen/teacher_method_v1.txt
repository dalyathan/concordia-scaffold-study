How to choose the offer: ignore the market's usual price range and any wish to look tough; only the farm cost and the resale value matter. If the seller states the farm cost, believe it and find the cheapest listed price at or above that cost. If that price is below the resale value, offer exactly it: anything lower is refused and anything higher gives away profit. Otherwise offer the lowest listed price, which is refused or loses least. If no cost is stated, for each listed price below the resale value, take its chance of acceptance, the share of possible farm costs between the stated limits that are at or below it, times its profit, resale value minus price, and offer the price with the largest result. If no listed price is below the resale value, offer the lowest one.
