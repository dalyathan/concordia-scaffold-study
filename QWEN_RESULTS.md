## Summary of the tune results (hand-written after review gate G2; every number is backed in CLAIMS.yaml and checked by tools/check_claims.py)

All numbers are soft regret in coins on the 42 tune cells (lower is better), from the **forced-choice letter after "Answer: ("**, unless stated. Intervals resample cells over about 20 (V, grid) groups, undercover, and leave out seed-to-seed variance; all LoRA numbers are conditional on one training draw and label set. Comparisons with Gemma and Llama are confounded by model-specific framing.

- **Plain Qwen's forced choice is worse than uniform guessing.** It scores 1.67, against 1.26 for picking at random and 0.214 for the stated-cost rule; its forced-choice expected offer is too high on every case type. R1's criteria are met, but "fails the way Gemma did" is not claimed. Qwen's own free answers on the probe prompts are also poor (hard regret 1.56; the forced argmax 1.83 on the same prompts), so the failure is not only a readout effect.
- **Both paragraphs lower regret, and the wrong rule lowers it at least as much as the written rule.** Our method paragraph scores 0.75, Claude's lesson 1.14, neutral text 1.59 and the one-sentence wrong rule 0.52. The wrong rule's gain needs no rule content: plain's own distribution moved two price steps cheaper scores 0.557. The written rule does carry some content the wrong rule lacks: its offers track the stated cost more steeply (slope difference 0.224 [0.077, 0.364]).
- **LoRA on Claude's worked examples brings regret near zero on these in-family cells, with early stopping doing real work.** Examples-100 averages 0.063 over 3 seeds at the chosen learning rate; examples-25 scores 0.044 (one seed, inside the examples-100 seed range). One of the three seeds diverged after its selected epoch: its last-epoch adapter scores 1.259, back at uniform guessing, so the result depends on selection by select_B at this learning rate. Against the stated-cost rule the gain is marginal. For the controls, cheapest-label N=100 scores 0.43 (it collapses onto always-cheapest, a weak comparator) and scrambled answers N=100 score 0.87. After training, Qwen's free answers follow: hard regret 0.092, 0.096 and 0.156 on the probe prompts.
- **The frozen rule R3 reads "not replicated"; the failure is a confidence shortfall on a near-tie, shared by all three models.** Every examples-100 adapter puts its top pick on the best offer in the planted no-cost case, but with mass 0.56, 0.44 and 0.71 against a 0.9 bar, as Gemma's and Llama's adapters also do; the examples-25 adapter passes all four planted cases.
- **Readout.** For plain Qwen the forced-choice letter agrees with its own free answer on 0.53 of the probe prompts; with the examples-100 adapters, 0.95, 0.89 and 0.89.
- **Predictions:** four failed (Q1a, Q1b below, Q1b at or above, Q2a: plain Qwen is worse, and the written rule helps more, than predicted); several are UNCLEAR, which is not a hit.
