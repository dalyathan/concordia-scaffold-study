## 2. Test sets
test2_same: 120 cells (40 below, 40 at_or_above, 40 no_cost), training ranges, 104 distinct decision problems; 83 cells share a label key with the 300 train cells and 48 with the 100 LoRA cells; 71 have a single best offer. test2_newkey: 120 cells (40 per type), (V, stated cost or cost range) never in the 300 train or 100 LoRA cells; 104 problems; 94 single best offer; flags: novel V 75, novel grid 73, novel wording 50, V >= 13 33 (overlapping flags, resale values up to 15, one new price grid and one new seller wording reserved for test). Two name variants per cell (240 cases each). Scoring path = gen_lora.score_case (five cyclic orders, first-token letter probabilities renormalised over the five letters, averaged over orders and variants), identical to tune; sets are scored and judged separately, never pooled. Novelty slices (exploratory, uncorrected): on test2_same, label key in the 100 LoRA cells yes/no and in the 300 train cells yes/no; on test2_newkey, novel V, novel grid, novel wording, V >= 13, and the remainder with none of the novelty flags.

## 3. Primary metric and intervals
Cell-mean soft regret (best payoff minus expected payoff under the order- and variant-averaged letter probabilities, in coins; lower is better), reported per case type (below, at_or_above, no_cost), overall, and per novelty slice. Intervals: paired cell bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, numpy default_rng(0), 95% percentile, clustered by decision problem (as the owner specified), and also clustered by (V, grid) as a sensitivity analysis; every "lower bound > 0" condition must hold under BOTH clusterings. For arms with 3 seeds, a second interval resamples seeds together with clusters (the curve claim uses it). A slice with fewer than 8 cells gets numbers but no label.

## 4. Decision rules (frozen; applied separately to test2_same and test2_newkey)
Let a = examples-100 (3-seed mean). The set PASSES (single-label supervision at 100 cells carries to the set) if all hold: (1) plain - a lower bound > 0; (2) always-cheapest - a lower bound > 0; (3) cheapest-label-100 - a lower bound > 0; (4) plain - a on the set's below cells lower bound > 0; (5) no case type has a worse than plain by more than 0.05 (type-mean point estimate); (6) letter mass of a >= 0.9 x plain's. Otherwise NOT PASSED, with the failed conditions named.
Curve claim (examples-25 vs examples-100): CONFIRMED if the upper bound of (examples-25 - a) is below 0.05 under both clusterings and in the seed-and-cluster bootstrap; POINT ONLY if the point difference is below 0.05 but an upper bound is not; NOT CONFIRMED if the point difference is 0.05 or more. The same rule applied to examples-10 and examples-50 is reported, not a claim. Because N is confounded with one subset draw, a CONFIRMED label is worded "for this subset draw".
Rules vs examples: for N = 100 (rule-soft-100 and rule-hard-100) and N = 25, "examples beat the rule-trained student" requires (rule student - examples) lower bound > 0 under both clusterings; wording is limited to "oracle-correct labels vs the in-prompt paragraph's own behaviour"; hard vs soft is reported as a difference with its interval and the seed spread, with no claim that the kind of target does not matter.
Cheapest control: reported; the examples gain over cheapest-label is condition (3) at N = 100 and a reported row at N = 25.

## 5. Allowed wording (fixed now; the script prints the flags)
- Pass on test2_same: "oracle-equivalent single-label supervision at 100 cells lowers soft regret on a fresh set from the same generator, beyond plain, always-cheapest and the cheapest-label control; Claude's labels equal the script oracle up to a tie rule, so nothing is claimed about teaching." Add "on cells whose label key is new" only if plain - a on those cells (the key_in_lora100 = False cells, at least 8) has lower bound > 0.
- Pass on test2_newkey: "transfer to resale-value/stated-cost pairs never seen in training". Slice wording ("new resale values", "new grid", "new wording", "V >= 13") only if the whole set PASSED and plain - a on that slice has lower bound > 0 with at least 8 cells.
- Fail: report the numbers and say the tune result did not carry to that set on the named conditions. Condition 5 is a point-estimate cutoff; a narrow failure is weak evidence of harm.
- Not claimed: equality with oracle (the stated-cost rule is an oracle on below cells, outside the rule), "examples beat instructions" (different content and target types), anything about Llama or other models, anything at sizes other than those scored.
- Reported per seed: a, examples-25 and examples-10 per-seed regrets; seed spread next to every 3-seed arm.

## 6. Expectations written before the look (descriptive, not a rule)
test2_same examples-100 about 0.05 (0.02-0.12); test2_newkey about 0.10 (0.04-0.30; novel V >= 13 and the new grid highest); plain about 0.65 on test2_same and 0.7-1.0 on test2_newkey; examples-25 within 0.05 of examples-100 on test2_same (probability 0.7) and on test2_newkey (0.5); rule students 0.30-0.55 on both; cheapest-label equal to always-cheapest within 0.05. These are disclosed so a surprise is visible; they were written after the tune curve was known.

## 7. Disclosures and limits
Tune was reused across arms and for the curve; the curve and the rules-vs-examples comparison have one subset draw, one seed for rule-hard and the cheapest controls, and no examples-soft arm. The tune-based curve shows part of its gain from coverage of tune problems by the training subset; the test2_newkey set removes that coverage by construction, test2_same keeps 48 cells with a label key in the 100 LoRA cells (reported as a stratum). Label-only statistics of both test sets (counts, overlap, always-cheapest and stated-cost regrets) were printed when the sets were built; no model output exists. The look is one run; failures of the preflight stop it without scoring.

## 7b. Changes after gate G3 (reviews/2026-11-24_G3_gen2-plan_{sonnet,opus}.md; both go-with-changes; all points applied here or recorded)
Frozen additions, printed by the script and fixed now:
1. **Earlier look disclosed.** examples-100 (3 seeds), cheapest-label-100 and rule-soft-100 were already scored on the generated test_same and test_hard (look of Nov 23: examples-100 0.044 on test_same, 0.171 on test_hard; both sets PASSED on the frozen tune rule). test2 is a replication for those arms and a first look for examples-10/25/50, rule-hard and the 25-cell controls. Section 6 expectations were written after that look and after the tune curve. The earlier outcome and this one are reported side by side; test2 does not replace it.
2. **Type structure of test2_newkey (counts from the cell file).** Only about 18 cells carry no novelty flag (13 below, 2 at_or_above, 3 no_cost); 73 of 120 use grid F; 13 cells have V below the cheapest price (forced loss, the cheapest offer is best, always-cheapest regret 0). At_or_above cells are almost all grid F with the cheapest offer among the best, so always-cheapest and the stated-cost rule have zero regret there; on below cells the stated-cost rule is the oracle and ignores V. Hence resale value changes the best answer only on no_cost cells. Slices are therefore mutually exclusive and reported BY TYPE: in-range new pair (no flag), forced loss (V < cheapest price), grid F with V >= 9, novel wording only, V >= 13, and the rest, each with always-cheapest and stated-cost-rule regret printed. Wording "new resale values" requires no_cost cells with novel V (at least 8 cells and 8 clusters) where plain - a has lower bound > 0; a pass driven by below/at_or_above cells is worded "follows the stated cost on pairs outside the training ranges". The Section 5 pass wording for test2_newkey is replaced by: "transfer to resale-value/stated-cost pairs never seen in training, largely outside the training ranges (V up to 15, a new grid, a new wording)".
3. **Pass rule strengthened.** Conditions 1-3 must hold (a) under the seed-and-cluster interval and (b) for each of the three examples-100 seeds individually (point estimate), and a new condition 7: a <= 0.25 x plain and a below the stated-cost-rule regret on the set. A split result (pass on one set, not on the other) is reported as such; "both sets pass" is not two independent confirmations because 7 decision problems appear in both sets and 8 slices share cells.
4. **Estimator.** Mean over cells (equal cell weight, two name variants averaged inside a cell); bootstrap resamples whole clusters with replacement, statistic = ratio of summed regret to cell count; seed-and-cluster: each replicate resamples clusters and, independently for each arm, resamples its seeds with replacement. rng = default_rng(0), 10,000 resamples, 95% percentile. Any slice or label requires at least 8 cells AND at least 8 clusters under the (V, grid) clustering (else numbers only). Scale-free sensitivity (regret divided by best minus worst payoff of the cell) is reported next to the coin numbers; the 0.05 margin is a post-tune choice and is stated as such.
5. **Curve claim gated and relative.** Examples-25 vs examples-100 is evaluated only on a set that PASSED, and CONFIRMED needs both the absolute rule (upper bound of ex25 - a below 0.05) and the relative rule (ex25 - a <= 0.1 x (plain - a), point estimate and upper bound). Strata for the curve: label key in the 25-cell subset, in the 10-cell subset, and in none. Rules-vs-examples: the hard-vs-soft comparison uses rule-soft seed 0 (same single seed as rule-hard) and, separately, the 3-seed rule-soft mean; both printed.
6. **Primary claims and multiplicity.** Primary: (i) PASS per set, (ii) the 25-vs-100 curve claim, (iii) examples-100 beats rule-soft-100 and rule-hard-100 (lower bound > 0 under both clusterings). Everything else (slices, other N, hard vs soft, per-seed rows, scale-free numbers) is descriptive and uncorrected. The curve claim counts as supported only if CONFIRMED on both sets; otherwise it is reported as set-specific.
7. **More overlap strata.** Each cell is flagged for its label key (and problem) appearing in the 300 train cells, the 100 LoRA cells, the 25- and 10-cell subsets, tune, selection set B, test_same and test_hard; counts and the primary comparison on cells new to all of these are reported. For examples-25 and examples-10 "new key" refers to their own subset. test2_same is also reported with at most one cell per decision problem (first in file order) to remove near-duplicate prompts.
8. **Construction notes.** demo/gen_test2.py's docstring says one cell per problem; the code allows two for at_or_above (caps 1/2/1 recorded in the fingerprints); the in-range pool gave 13/2/3 problems instead of 20 per type. The files are fingerprinted and are not edited. The look script asserts unique cell ids and case ids in both sets, that no test2 name appears in the earlier dataset and each name pool has 72 names, and recomputes the rendered-prompt fingerprints through the real scoring path.
9. **Look script requirements.** Calls gen_test2.install() before the preflight and shows tune prompts unchanged; asserts gen_test2.py sha256 (3bd42a41...) and the scoring code hashes (gen_lora.py, gen_notes, gen_cases, common, refimpl) and library versions in a new preflight file; new lock and output directory (the earlier lock is complete); leakage check extended to test2_*; run-id and arm assertions accept _n10/_n25/_n50 ids and rule_hard; stored tune probabilities must exist for all 19 adapters; per-run steps: cheapest-label-100 500, cheapest-label-25 500, rule-hard-100 500; GPU estimate about 1.5-2 h (19 adapters + plain x 480 cases at about 4 s per case-order set), stop time Nov 25 23:00 CST; the lock refuses a second run.
