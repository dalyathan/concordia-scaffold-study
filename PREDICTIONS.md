## Scoring rule (mechanical; implemented in demo/gen_curve_report.py; applied after G2 without discretion)
A point prediction with a range: HELD if the observed point estimate (mean over the arm's seeds that exist) lies inside the range, FAILED if outside. A comparison prediction (A beats B with lower bound > 0): HELD if the point difference lies inside its stated range and the lower bound of the (V, grid)-clustered paired bootstrap (the wider interval) is > 0; FAILED if the point difference is outside the range or has the wrong sign; UNCLEAR if the point is inside the range but that lower bound is <= 0. Threshold claims ("within 0.05", "below 0.25") are point-estimate rules unless marked; a ratio with a denominator below 0.10 is UNCLEAR. A run that was cut by the budget guard makes its prediction UNCLEAR (a partly run N = 50 is scored on the seeds that exist and flagged). Probability statements are scored by the Brier score (listed, not held / failed). A single-seed difference smaller than the seed spread of examples-100 or rule-soft-100 (max minus min of per-run tune regret) is reported next to the verdict as noise-sized.
