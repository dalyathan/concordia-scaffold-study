How to choose the offer. First check whether the seller has said what the fruit cost them at the farm. If a cost is stated and it is below the resale value, offer exactly that cost: the seller accepts it, any higher offer only gives away profit, and any lower offer is refused. If the stated cost is at or above the resale value, no accepted offer can make a profit, so offer the lowest possible price. If no cost is stated, every farm cost is equally likely; for each possible offer, multiply the chance that it is accepted (the share of possible costs at or below it) by the profit it would leave (resale value minus offer), and choose the offer with the largest result.
