# G1 review, Sonnet: LoRA plan (distillation arms with matched controls; 11 train / 6 held-out tune cells)

**Verdict: go-with-changes.** The code looks correct and the leakage guards are sound. The pass rule and the controls are too weak for a pass to mean "the method's content lives in the weights". The budget plan also fails its own 10-hour limit.

1. **The pass rule mostly tests "better than plain".** Epoch 0 (B=0, equal to plain) is a candidate best epoch in every run. Neutral and wrong_rule teachers are no better than plain on the train cells (0.755, 0.819 vs 0.778), so their students sit at or near plain, and beating them is close to beating plain. Missing comparators: const2 gets 0.333 on the held-out cells, so a student recovering less than about 60% of the plain-to-method gap (0.577 to 0.171) loses to a constant. answer_only and shuffled are absent from the rule. Fix: add const2 and the shuffled arms as required comparators (lower bound above 0); report answer_only alongside; add an effect-size floor (e.g. at least 50% of the teacher gap); compare the student to its own teacher, the in-prompt method row (0.171).
2. **The held-out unit is too small and lumpy.** 6 cells, signal in 3 (the at-or-above-resale cell has plain regret 0.002, the no-cost cells about 0.11). A percentile bootstrap on 6 cells is poorly calibrated. Training never contains stated cost 4 or best offer 4; the held-out cell (5, C 4) needs offer 4, so it tests extrapolation by one cell. Fix: per-cell paired differences (a sign count out of 6) beside the interval; paired resampling across arms; state that a null is uninformative at this size.
3. **Selection on the held-out cells makes the pass rule in-sample** (up to 3 learning rates, up to 8 epochs, best-epoch pick on the same 6 cells). Fix: fix the epoch and rate rule from training-cell loss before looking, or call the result a go/no-go gate only; never quote it as evidence.
4. **The trainability check reads the wrong epoch.** `train_best` is the training regret at the epoch where held-out regret was best. If answer_only generalises poorly its best held-out epoch is early, training regret is still high, and the fallback fires wrongly. The check is also nearly vacuous (memorising 11 label sets says nothing about the method distillation being learnable). Fix: use the minimum or final training regret over the history; add the method student's loss against its own teacher.
5. **Letter mass and free-text transfer are only logged, not gated.** The steering rung failed on mass collapse. Training on the "Answer: (" readout could learn a readout-specific shortcut; the free-text check is the real "in the weights" test. Fix: require held-out mass not to fall below plain; make free-text hard regret on the held-out cases a co-primary outcome with a threshold.
6. **The budget plan overruns and the reduction rule is not what the proposal says.** Full plan 27 grid + 6 extra-seed runs = 33 runs, about 19.7 h at 330 s per epoch and 6.5 epochs; each reduction step still exceeds 10 h (17.3, 15.5, 13.7); the code silently picks the last variant. Fix: more reduction steps (shuffled with 1 permutation, a restricted rate grid for the non-core arms); make the driver refuse to run over budget. The 6.5 epochs and 330 s per epoch are unmeasured; model load, teacher passes and extra evaluations are not counted.
7. **The seed asymmetry biases the comparison.** Under "controls get 1 seed", method is averaged over 3 seeds and the controls over 1; averaging lowers variance and the effect of best-epoch selection, which favours method. Fix: compare per seed, or give every compared arm the same seed count, or drop the pass claim in the reduced plan. Seed variance is not in the bootstrap.
8. **Smaller.** wrong_rule is called same-length matched but is about 7 tokens against about 150 for the method paragraph (a content control, not a length control). The fallback (rank 16) and the later rank-4 lr 1e-3 run share a log filename (`rid` omits rank). Check 1 demands exact equality; define a tolerance in advance. Check 4 (adapter-keyed caches) is listed but not implemented in `lora_checks.py`.

Verified: target ordering is consistent; teachers are computed before the PEFT wrap; the cells are split cleanly with all four variants in one split; development-only asserts are present; the derangement logic is correct; gradient accumulation (loss/5 over 5 orders, one step per case) is correct. Not verified: `refimpl` internals, no_grad in scoring, all timing figures; no run outputs exist.

Freeze before the single test look: the pass rule (comparators, effect-size floor, bootstrap details, mass and free-text gates) written before any training result is seen; one adapter per arm chosen by a stated rule (rate, epoch, whether seeds are ensembled); the metrics (test plain 0.534; primary soft regret by cell; secondary mass, free-text regret, hard regret; comparison with the in-prompt method row); the arms, targets, teacher caches and code hashes; a statement that the test cells are evaluated once; what counts as a positive, a null or "undertrained", and what a tune-cell pass licenses (spending the one test look) versus what a fail means.
