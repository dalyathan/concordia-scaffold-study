# G2 review, Sonnet: steering verdict (per-order cheap is a letter push; order-averaged vectors do not carry the method text)

**Verdict: partly justified.** The empirical findings hold up. The mechanistic story in items 2–4 is stronger than the evidence, and one number in the proposal contradicts its own table.

1. **Item 4 contradicts the results it explains.** It says P1 vectors "built from earlier layers" cannot carry the method text, but the order-averaged vectors were built at layers 27–39, where patching the P1 residual recovers 50–90% of the effect and the same-layer mean-difference vectors do almost nothing (sentence −0.07; +0.26 at layer 39, wrong sign). So the failure is that a case- and order-averaged unit direction does not capture the case- and order-specific patch delta (method-vs-plain log-prob differences run from 1.3 to 7.9 nats and vary by order). The sentence vector raises letter mass ×1.2–1.4 (the method text ×1.36), so it seems to capture the format component, not the content. Fix (cheap): steer with the actual delta st_method[L] − st_plain[L] in three forms (per case and order; averaged over orders within a case; averaged over cases within an order); also run per-order sentence vectors, the analogue of (a). Drop the "late attention" wording unless K/V or attention outputs are patched.
2. **The "letter push, not valuation" test cannot discriminate.** A per-case correlation of 0.987–0.999 with an unembedding push is expected for any push that lowers the 1-coin letter's logit (a ceiling/headroom effect). The matched push uses five fractions and is not matched in mean (L36: −0.84 vs −1.04; L33: −0.64 vs −0.50). Fixes: a wrong-order control (steer order oi with `cheap_order[(oi+1)%5]`); regress per-case dE on plain P(1-coin letter) and compare residuals; report the cosine of each per-order vector with its own unembedding difference. The method text's own effect is also order-dependent, so "letter-specific" does not separate steering from text.
3. **The free-text number is wrong and cuts against item 3.** The proposal says "3 of 4 changed from 2 coins to 1"; the table shows two of four changed (v15-V3-information-C4 and v15-V5-pressure); case C2 stayed at 1 and only its wording changed. The setting came from (a)'s grid but was applied to the (b) vector. Fix: free text on all 12 cases, and on the letter-free prompt; share moved per case type; a random-direction control.
4. **The statistics are too thin.** Partial Spearman at n = 12 (6 cells) with no interval; +0.10 to +0.21 against −0.02 sits inside the spread of the matched pushes (+0.18, +0.04, −0.29); plain does not track cost either. "Hard regret does not improve" rests on 6 cases. Selecting the best setting on the same 12 cases with 3 random controls is a weak null. Fix: add norm-matched shuffled-case and wrong-order controls; cell-level bootstrap intervals; state these as descriptive and drop partial Spearman as evidence.
5. **Item 1's "positive finding" is trivially satisfiable.** Plain overbids (expected offer 2.34, best offers about 1–2), so any downward push reduces regret; per-order steering also uses knowledge of the option order. Fix: say "recorded as a letter-readout effect", not "positive finding".
6. **The grid is limited.** (b) covers layers 27–39 at P1 only, fractions up to 1.0; the earlier scan used 2.0, and P3-onward steering lowered offers by up to −0.36 with mass dropping. "Cannot reproduce the effect" is limited to P1-only mean-difference vectors; the title and the earlier excerpt ("steering cannot move offers") are broader than the evidence.
7. **Positive control unvalidated.** Nowhere is it shown that the "cheap" text (`paragraph(case,1)` against `paragraph(case,4)`) lowers offers when placed in the prompt on these cases. Cheapest check: score those two texts as notes on the 12 cases.

Code notes (could not verify): no bug found that changes the numbers; `phase3.steering_hook` and `refimpl` were not provided; layer-41 steering acts before the final RMSNorm and softcap, so unit(W_U diff) is not exactly a logit-space direction (minor); the 0.9×plain threshold is arbitrary; `resid[41]` comes from 4 cases.

Freeze before the single test look: whether anything steering-related goes to test; the coherence rule and primary metric; the control set (negated, wrong-order, matched unembedding push, norm-matched random); the statistical unit (cells) and interval method; the test-case list; the final verdict wording, narrowed to "P1-only order-averaged mean-difference vectors at layers 12–39 did not reproduce the method text; per-order vectors gave a letter-readout effect not shown to be content-sensitive".
