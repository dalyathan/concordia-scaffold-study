# G2 review, Fable: steering verdict (per-order cheap is a letter push; order-averaged vectors do not carry the method text)

**Verdict: partly justified.** The scoped negative holds on the tune cases: order-averaged P1 mean-difference vectors do not reproduce the method text. The "letter-readout push, not a valuation signal" reading is plausible but rests on non-discriminating evidence. Point 4 over-reads the patching, "real but weak" is unsupported, and three summary numbers do not match the data. Every proposal figure was recounted against `steer_checks.json` and `steer_content_check.json`; all match except those flagged.

1. **The 0.987–0.999 correlation does not discriminate (point 2).** At layer 39, fraction 1.0 the steered expected offer is 1.07, so every case is at the floor and the per-case change is roughly 1 minus the plain offer for any strong downward push. At unsaturated settings the push is poorly matched in mean (−0.64 vs −0.50 at layer 33; −0.84 vs −1.04 at layer 36; fraction 0.1 reused). There is no reference for a content-sensitive intervention; the method text or the layer-36 patch might correlate just as highly through headroom alone. Fix: add the method-text and layer-33/36 patch per-case changes to the same comparison; report the correlation with the plain expected offer; match the push by bisection; compare per-order letter distributions.
2. **The direct test of "the letter that stands for 1 coin" was not run.** Near-orthogonality across orders is indirect; the top-10 tokens are for order 0 only, where "a", "first option" and "1 coin" coincide. Fix: steer order i with order j's vector (the claim predicts mass moves to the letter meaning 1 coin in order j); report the cosine of each per-order vector with the matching unembedding difference.
3. **The partial Spearman and hard-regret bullets are underpowered.** 12 cases from 6 cells; 6 cost-below-resale cases, so 0.67 vs 0.83 is one case; the permutation p-value is computed and discarded; no method-text value shows what "tracks" looks like; the push's own values run from +0.18 to −0.29. Fix: report p-values or cell-level bootstrap intervals and the method-text reference; word it as "no evidence of tracking".
4. **"The directions are real but weak" (point 3) is not supported.** The negated runs are incoherent by the proposal's own criterion (letter mass 0.65 of plain for cheap, 0.03 for sentence), which the proposal omits; renormalising over 3% of the mass drifts toward uniform (expected offer 3), which alone gives a rise from 2.34. Fix: drop the claim, or negate at a coherent fraction.
5. **The sentence vector is not weak; the order-averaged metric hides what it does.** At layer 33, fraction 1.0, order 0 puts 0.78–0.97 on "a" in all four cases (plain mostly "b"), yet the order-averaged change is −0.07: a fixed-letter push that cancels over cyclic orders; regret there is 0.606, worse than plain. The "best" setting is picked by change in expected offer (a tie with layer 27: −0.0719 vs −0.0712); the best sentence regret is 0.560. Fix: select and report on regret; save per-order results (only aggregates are saved).
6. **Point 4 over-reads the patching.** The null before layer 24 is solid for the natural P1 state. Recovery at layers 33–39 is per-case and per-order and may just copy the answer letter. It does not explain why the mean vector fails at layers 27–39 where the patch works: at its natural norm (about 0.24) the sentence vector at layer 33 gives −0.015 against −0.35 for the patch, i.e. the content is case- and order-specific, not "too early". Recovery jumps at layers 28, 33, 36, 39 with flat stretches between (unexplained). Fix: reword to "the natural P1 state before about layer 24 is insufficient"; test per-order and per-case-type sentence vectors; attention is untested.
7. **The free-text observation is misreported and comes from a code accident.** Two of four cases changed from 2 to 1, not three of four. In `steer_checks.py` extras, `best_all` is check (a)'s per-order setting but the vector applied is the order-averaged cheap one, a pairing never chosen (in table (b) it raises the letter-readout offer by 0.19). Taken at face value it cuts against verdict sentence 1: the order-averaged vector may carry "1 coin" when the readout is a number rather than a letter. Fix: free text on all 12 cases with negated and random controls, or scope the verdict to the first-token letter readout.
8. **Three reporting errors.** "13 of 40 with letter mass at or above plain": 13 holds only at the 0.9 coherence threshold (10 at or above plain); the correlation range is 0.980–0.999 (layer 36 is 0.980); per-order steering at its best (0.407) is still worse than always-2 (0.333).
9. **Weak controls.** Three isotropic random directions from one seed; they alone moved letter mass to between 0.64 and 1.18 of plain at layer 39. Fix: mismatched-order vectors and random directions within the five-letter unembedding span.
10. **Minor.** `steer_check_passes` has `A and B or C` precedence that does not match its stated rule (no effect here); plain puts 0.21–0.45 of first-token mass on "(", which steering shifts and the renormalised readout ignores.

Not verifiable from the packet: `phase3.steering_hook`, `lm.letter_scores`, `core.*`, `steercheck.partial_spearman`; hooks verified only at layer 41; the positive-finding clause definition, the 0.508 and 0.333 references and the cosine 0.71 are not in the folder.
